WHAT THE EVIDENCE POINTS TO / 04

What makes a viewer
stay or move on?

These are research themes to test against the full interview dataset, not ranked findings or population-wide claims.

A / STAY
▤

Content that fits the habit

A broad mix can serve viewers who switch between films, series and other genres. A specialist library can matter more to anime-first viewers.

B / SWITCH
↗

A title they cannot find

Regional catalogue gaps can make a second platform feel necessary, even when the viewer likes their current service.

C / TRUST
◌

The promise must hold

Reviews supplied with the project mention playback, ads and membership frustrations. A broken promise can weaken perceived value.
